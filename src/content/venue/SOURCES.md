# Venue photo sources

The venue cards on `/` (`src/content/venue.json`) use public photos of Bloom Tower
(Av. Javier Prado Oeste 2501 / Flora Tristán 310, Magdalena del Mar), found through Google Images.
They are third-party photos: the copyright stays with each author. Replace them with our own
photos of the 16th floor (or confirm permission with the authors) before the event.

| File | Shows | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `facade.jpg` | Front facade | DLPS Arquitectos, building architects: <https://dlpsarquitectos.com/proyecto/edificio-bloom-tower/> (`bloom_tower_01`) |
| `corner.jpg` | Corner view from Javier Prado Oeste | DLPS Arquitectos, same page (`bloom_tower_02`) |
| `training-room.jpg` | Training room (not confirmed to be the 16th floor) | Lumicenter, lighting supplier: <https://lumicenter.com.pe/portfolio-item/bloom-tower/> (`proyectos_lumicenter_bloom_tower_04`) |
| `common-area.jpg` | Shared dining / common area (not confirmed to be the 16th floor) | Lumicenter, same page (`proyectos_lumicenter_bloom_tower_02`) |

The DLPS photos were converted from WebP to JPEG and scaled to 1600 px on the long side.
The Lumicenter photos are unchanged.
